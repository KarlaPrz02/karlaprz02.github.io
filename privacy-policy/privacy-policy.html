Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how this Discord bot (“JuniBot”) collects, uses, and handles information when you interact with or use the Bot.

1. Information We Collect

The Bot may process certain information provided by Discord that is necessary for its functionality, including:

  • Discord User IDs
  • Discord Server IDs
  • Channel IDs
  • Message IDs and message content when required to execute commands
  • Usernames or display names when required for specific features

The Bot does not intentionally collect sensitive personal information such as passwords, payment information, home addresses, or telephone numbers.

2. How We Use Information

Information processed by the Bot may be used to:

  • Provide and operate the Bot's features.
  • Execute commands requested by users.
  • Maintain and improve the Bot.
  • Prevent abuse, spam, or misuse.
  • Troubleshoot technical problems.

The Bot only processes information that is reasonably necessary for its intended functionality.

3. Data Storage

Depending on the Bot's features, certain information may be temporarily or permanently stored.

Any stored information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the relevant functionality or maintain the Bot.

We do not intentionally retain personal information that is not necessary for the operation of the Bot.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or intentionally share users' personal information with third parties.

Information may only be disclosed when necessary to operate a specific feature, comply with a legal obligation, or protect the security and integrity of the Bot.

5. Discord

The Bot operates through Discord and is subject to Discord's own Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

Discord may independently collect and process information about your use of Discord and the Bot. This processing is governed by Discord's policies and is outside the control of the Bot's developer.

6. Data Deletion

If you believe that the Bot stores information associated with your Discord account and would like that information deleted, you may contact the Bot's developer.

Requests will be reviewed and, where applicable, stored information will be deleted unless it is required to be retained for legitimate technical or legal reasons.

7. Children's Privacy

The Bot is not designed to intentionally collect personal information from children.

If you believe that the Bot has collected personal information from a child without appropriate authorization, please contact the developer so the matter can be addressed.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to the Bot, its features, or the way information is processed.

Any changes will be published on this page, along with the updated date.

9. Contact

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or the Bot's handling of information, you may contact the Bot's developer through Discord.

By using the Bot, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.
